第一集 (EPISODE 1)

1-1 索恩狼族庄园 密室 深夜 室内

（字幕：索恩狼族领地 - Alpha庄园）

△（快切） 密室。石墙上雕刻着古老的狼族图腾。烛光昏暗。

△ 凯厄斯·索恩（CAIUS THORNE，30岁，深色西装） 双膝跪地，胸口如火烧般灼热，强行压制着体内翻涌的力量。

[VFX] 金色的纹路从他的手背一直蔓延到手臂。几名手下被震飞出去，重重地撞在墙上。

△ 奥德里克·赛拉斯（ALDRIC SILAS，狼族长老，60多岁） 走进来，看都没看一眼倒在地上的手下直接跨了过去。他在距离凯厄斯三步远的地方停下：

Aldric Silas (low, grave): Alpha. The suppressant injections have reached their limit. The next full moon — is the deadline.

△ 凯厄斯站起身，整理了一下西装，拍掉膝盖上的灰尘。他的金色双眸燃烧着微光。双手——仍在轻微颤抖。

Caius (voice ice-cold): Your point.

Aldric Silas: You need to find your fated pure-blood Omega. She's the only one who can save you. But Alpha — it will cost her life.

△ 凯厄斯猛地攥紧椅背。指节泛白。

Caius (heavy): Pure-blood Omegas are nearly extinct — Finding mine... we've been searching for ten years.

Aldric Silas: Soon, Alpha. In recent months I have sensed the presence of your fated Omega drawing closer.

△ 但丁（DANTE，助理，28岁） 敲门进入：

Dante (low): Alpha — we have a lead. On your fated Omega. But Adrian and your stepmother... they're searching too.

△ 凯厄斯的眼神瞬间变得凌厉。

Caius: A lead? It seems God is still on my side.

Caius (cold laugh, no warmth): They still dreaming of taking my place. How pathetic.

Caius (cont., voice flat): The position of Alpha King belongs to me. Only mine. At any cost. The life of one mere Omega... means nothing.

Caius (turning to Dante): Expand the search. Watch for a crescent-shaped moon mark on the lower back. And keep a close eye on Adrian and the others.

△ 但丁退出。奥德里克注视着凯厄斯的背影：

Aldric Silas (barely containing his relief): Thank the God. The wolf clan will be saved.

[VFX] 凯厄斯手背上的深金色纹路微弱地闪烁了一下——随后被压制下去。

1-2 小镇 街道 黄昏 室外

（字幕：一个小镇）

△ 夕阳将街道染成一片金黄。艾里斯·温（IRIS WYNN，21岁） 穿着外卖制服走在路灯下，一手提着蛋糕盒，一手拿着手机，边走边笑。

Best Friend (on the phone, excited): Iris! Medical school! You got into medical school!

Iris: If Mom and Dad could see this... They'd be happy, wouldn't they.

Best Friend (voice softening): They're watching from heaven. They must be so proud of you.

Iris (quietly): Medical school... I think my life won't feel so lonely anymore.

△（特写） 镜头平移到她脖子上的项链——一个古老的狼族图腾。这是她父母留下的遗物。

Best Friend (laughing): I hope you find someone in college who loves you. You've been so strong all these years — but I know how much you've longed for that. Someone to belong to. A family.

Iris: Yeah. I really have.

△（慢动作） 一辆黑色越野车高速冲过街角。艾里斯为了保护蛋糕盒猛地侧身，重重地摔在地上。

[VFX] 她后腰的衣服裂开。一个新月形状的标记在血迹中显现——发出一闪而过的银色微光。

△ 越野车后窗内，凯厄斯的金色瞳孔猛然收缩。他探身向前，死死盯着那个月亮标记。

Caius (low, utterly controlled): It's her! My fated pure-blood Omega. Follow her.

△ 艾里斯手忙脚乱地爬起来，整理好衣服，转身离开——完全没有察觉。

Iris (letting out a breath): Oh, thank God — the cake is fine.

△ 但丁在后视镜里发现了什么：

Dante (low): Alpha — someone's following us. Adrian's car.

△ 凯厄斯没有回头。他慢慢靠回椅背：

Caius (cold smile, very quiet): Let him watch. We pull back first. I want to see exactly what my stepmother and brother are planning this time.

△ 画面淡出黑屏。
