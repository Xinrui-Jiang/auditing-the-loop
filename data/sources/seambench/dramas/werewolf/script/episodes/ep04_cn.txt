第四集 (EPISODE 4)

4-1 索恩庄园 艾里斯的卧室 深夜 室内

△ 艾里斯坐在床沿，手机贴在耳边，正和闺蜜热切地聊着。

Iris: There's a werewolf who helped me... and he said he wants to marry me.

Best Friend (on the phone, instantly hooked): Wait wait wait. A werewolf? Is he hot? Is he rich?

△ 艾里斯愣住了。她的脸慢慢涨红。

（闪回）

△ 松树林中。凯厄斯孤身迎战群狼——深金色纹路燃烧，每一次重击都带着毁灭性的力量，衬衫被血浸透，清晰地勾勒出他完美的身体线条。

（闪回结束）

Iris (flustered, voice dropping): ...Yeah. Hot. Really good body.

Best Friend (pressing): Okay but is he rich?

△ 艾里斯举起手机，环顾这间宽敞的卧室——落地窗，以及窗外广阔的林地和庄园：

Iris (slowly): I'm at his place right now... it seems... pretty big.

Best Friend (shrieking): WHAT! You're already living together!

Iris (quickly explaining): It's not like that. He's letting me stay here for now because the werewolf is after me — it's too dangerous to go out.

Iris (cont., turning serious): But marriage... I just want to go to school. Build a real life in the human world. At least I won't end up like every Omega before me — nothing but a breeding tool. I'll talk to him. Find another way to repay him.

Best Friend (voice softening): Maybe he's your fated Alpha? Maybe not every werewolf uses Omegas be the breeding tool?

△ 艾里斯沉默了片刻。她的手指抚摸着项链。

Iris (quietly, carrying something heavy): I don't know. I've always known who I am — what I am. My parents... died protecting me. If I end up walking the same road every Omega before me walked...

Best Friend (gently cutting in): Hey... just talk to him. He saved you. I think he'll understand.

△ 门开了。凯厄斯大步走进门口。

△ 艾里斯迅速挂断电话，站起身，深吸了一口气：

Iris (looking at him directly, serious): Hi Caius, I want to talk to you. I'm truly grateful you saved me. But I still don't want to join the werewolf clan. My parents gave their lives to protect me. Thank you for your help — but from here I'll be more careful on my own.

△ 还没等她说完——

△ 凯厄斯三步跨过房间，一把将她拉入怀中，狠狠地吻住了她——急切、压抑，如同某种被禁锢太久的东西终于爆发。

△ 艾里斯用力推他，双手抵在他胸前，将他推开：

Iris (pushing him off, catching her breath, shaken): What — what are you doing!

△ 凯厄斯站在原地，胸口剧烈起伏，一言不发。只是死死盯着她。

△ 画面淡出黑屏。
