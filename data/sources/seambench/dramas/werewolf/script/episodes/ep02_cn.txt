第二集 (EPISODE 2)

2-1 镇外松树林 别墅门廊 深夜 室外

△ 艾里斯提着外卖盒，独自穿过漆黑的松树林，嘴里哼着小曲，对身后逼近的人影毫无察觉。

△ 她将蛋糕盒放在别墅台阶上，用外卖软件拍照，转身准备离开——几个人影从树林边缘走了出来。

△ 阿德里安（ADRIAN，28岁，英俊，带着令人不安的微笑） 走上前，上下打量着她：

Adrian: Hi, sweetie. Walking alone at night... aren't you scared?

Iris (stepping back, on guard): Oh, just doing my job. Order is delivered. I'm leaving now.

Adrian (closing in, voice dropping): A pure-blood Omega doing food delivery in the human world? Interesting.

Iris (V.O.): How does he know what I am?

Iris (keeping her composure): I have no idea what you're talking about. If you need delivery, call my restaurant. I'm off the clock.

Adrian: Hi, sweetie — did I say you could go?

△ 阿德里安现在直接挡在了她面前。他的视线落在她腰侧——那个月亮标记上。他的笑意加深。他伸手想去挑她的下巴——艾里斯扭头躲开。

Adrian (low laugh): A pure-blood Omega with an attitude?

△ 艾里斯狠狠咬住他手背。阿德里安猛地缩回手，看了看血迹，笑了起来：

Adrian (low laugh): Ha. Okay, okay. Now I really want to have some fun with you.

△ 阿德里安抬起手。一名手下出现在艾里斯身后，捂住了她的口鼻。艾里斯剧烈挣扎——随后失去意识。

2-2 黑色越野车 同时 室内

△ 但丁倾身靠近凯厄斯：

Dante (low): Alpha — Adrian's men have taken the Omega, just as we expected.

△ 凯厄斯抬起眼睛。露出一抹冰冷、笃定的微笑。

Caius (voice dropping): Time to go pay my dear brother a visit... and meet that Omega.

2-3 松树林空地 深夜 室外

△ 艾里斯醒来。古老的狼族图腾柱围成一圈，火炬燃烧着。变身的狼群从四面八方将她包围。

△ 艾里斯慢慢坐起来，环顾四周。

△ 阿德里安站在中心，西装笔挺，神态极其从容：

Adrian: Don't be afraid, darling. I won't hurt you.

Iris (calm, laced with sarcasm): You kidnap me and tell me not to be afraid?

△ 艾里斯本能地伸手摸向自己的项链。

Adrian (stepping closer, tone shifting colder): You know what you are — a pure-blood Omega. Your parents gave their lives to hide you in the human world, thinking they could protect you.

△ 阿德里安俯下身，脸逼近她。

Adrian (cont.): And yet — here you are. I found you.

△ 艾里斯紧紧攥住项链，迎上他的目光：

Iris (furious): So? What is it you want to say? You kidnapped me, exposed my identity — and then what?

Iris: Werewolf clans worship strength. Omegas with no power are disposable. How many Omegas have been used and discarded? Treated as nothing but breeding tools their entire lives, forgotten without even a name. We chose to live among humans — because only humans treat us as equals. As people.

△ 阿德里安的笑容消失了。他的眼神变得冰冷。

Adrian (slow clap): Beautifully said. Quite the speech — I almost want to applaud. You think you get to choose? Should I call you naive... or just stupid.

△ 阿德里安挥手让手下上前。艾里斯不断后退，直到背靠在一棵松树上，退无可退。她深吸一口气——

[VFX] 银色的能量开始从艾里斯的指尖渗出。

△（慢动作） 一个黑影从树顶坠落。是凯厄斯。他的金色双眼猛然睁开，一圈金色的火焰从他体内辐射而出，定制西装下的肌肉紧绷。他降落在艾里斯和狼群之间——脚下的地面随之碎裂。

△ 凯厄斯直起身，俯视着阿德里安：

Caius (voice like ice): Long time no see, brother.

△ 阿德里安的手下本能地后退了一步。
