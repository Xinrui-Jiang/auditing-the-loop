第十五集 (EPISODE 15)

15-1 小镇医院 走廊 深夜 连续 室内

△ 两人站在走廊上。西奥还在另一端忙碌，没有看向这边。

△ 凯厄斯开口了——声音被刻意压低，但透着某种不屈的执拗：

Caius (restrained, unyielding): Come back to me. I will cure you, I swear it on my name as Caius Thorne!

△ 艾里斯看着他。她发出一声短促的轻笑——比愤怒更冷：

Iris (cold laugh, each word clear): You want to talk about taking responsibility. Now?

△ 她停顿了一下。直视着他的眼睛。一字一顿：

Iris (cont., one word at a time): Where was that responsibility when you were using me to cure yourself? You knew it was killing me!

△ 凯厄斯——僵住了。
Caius：What... what are you talking about?

△ 艾里斯转过头。她不想看他的脸。她的声音变得毫无起伏——就像某种被彻底压扁的东西：

Iris (back to him, voice even): You used me. I don't blame you for it — that was your instinct. That was your fate.

Caius: Who told you that?!
△ 她停顿了一下：

Iris (愤怒，cont.): But I have my own fate too. I don't owe you anything. And you don't owe me anything.

△ 在走廊的另一端，但丁大步走过来，凑近凯厄斯：

Dante (low, grave): Alpha — unknown wolf signatures have appeared in the town. Whereabouts unknown.

△ 艾里斯僵住了。

△ 凯厄斯察觉到了。他转过头：

Caius (watching her): What's wrong?

△ 艾里斯没有回答。她转身快步走开。

△ 凯厄斯上前一步挡住她：

Caius (blocking her, voice dropping): Calm down. Talk to me —

△ 艾里斯猛地转过身。

△ 她的眼睛红了。

△ 六年来第一次，她的声音在他面前崩溃了。

Iris (eyes red, voice breaking for the first time): Please — let me go!

△ 停顿了一瞬。像是在做一个决定。然后脱口而出：

Iris (cont., voice unsteady): I need to get back to my daughter.

△ 凯厄斯呆立在原地。

Caius (hoarse, rooted to the spot): ...Your daughter?
