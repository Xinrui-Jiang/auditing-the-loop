EPISODE 10: THE ILLUSION 
EXT. GREY ESTATE GARDENS - AFTERNOON 
Pure, echoing laughter of a child. 
Sun-drenched lawns sprawl like a fairytale. 
Victor, in casual white linen shirt, sleeves rolled, chases 
Milo across grass. Catches him, lifts high — Milo giggles 
wildly. 
Julian sits on stone bench nearby. Sun softens his features. 
He watches, mesmerized, a tender smile slipping out. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(longing) 
I grew up with nothing. No parents. 
No home. 
A family… this was all I ever 
dreamed of. 
Milo spots Julian, wiggles free, grabs his hand. 
MILO 
(excited tug) 
Mama Ju! Come play with Dada and 
me! 
Julian lets himself be pulled to grass. Laughs brightly, 
spins Milo — loses footing. 
Strong arm wraps Julian’s waist, catching him. Julian looks 
up into Victor’s eyes — tender, suffocating warmth. Victor 
brushes hair behind Julian’s ear. 
VICTOR 
(soft) 
I’ve got you. I’ll always catch 
you.17. 
Warm music cuts to sharp HELICOPTER WHIR. 
Julian snaps out. 
Beyond Victor, black helicopter lands. Butlers and executives 
wait in suits — power crashing like ice. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
But he’s Victor Grey. Owns half the 
city. 
And I… I’m just a toy he bought 
when I was starving. 
Light dies in Julian’s eyes. He steps back, arms wrapped 
defensively, face cold mask. 
JULIAN 
(hollow, looking away) 
I should go check on my patients. 
Julian walks quickly to mansion. Victor stands alone, hand 
dropping. Warmth vanishes — eyes storm dark, watching him go.
