EPISODE 5: THE BIRTHDAY PARTY 
INT. GREY ESTATE - BALLROOM - NIGHT 
Warm gold light shimmers from crystal chandeliers. 
High-society guests murmur over champagne. A string quartet 
hums softly.9. 
JULIAN, in a plain dark suit, lurks in the shadows, clutching 
a flute. His eyes dart frantically, searching for Milo amid 
the crowd. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(breath ragged, whispering 
anxiety) 
Get in, drop the gift, get out. 
This isn’t my world… and he can’t 
see me. 
The music swells subtly, building unease. Julian’s grip 
tightens — the glass stem creaks. 
A sharp GLASS CLINK pierces the air. The room hushes. Quartet 
fades to silence. 
Spotlight slices the grand staircase. Shadows stretch long as 
VICTOR GREY, exuding lethal maturity, descends step by 
deliberate step — an emperor claiming his throne. 
VICTOR 
(deep, echoing via mic, 
each word weighted) 
Thank you for coming… Four years 
ago, my life changed forever… 
when my son, Milo, entered the 
world. 
Julian’s breath catches. He stares up. The champagne trembles 
violently, nearly spilling. 
Sound drops: Only Julian’s THUMPING HEARTBEAT echoes, growing 
louder, faster. A faint RINGING builds in his ears. 
QUICK, JAGGED FLASHBACKS (POV): 
Milo’s watery eyes, pleading and familiar. 
Victor’s cold silhouette vanishing in the penthouse door 
four years ago. 
The gold-foiled “GREY” — a damning signature. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(voice cracking in 
despair) 
Four years… Milo is three. 
Oh god… he IS his son. 
What have I done? 
Panic surges. Julian edges back slowly, breath shallow, half
melting into the shadow of a massive Roman pillar. His hand 
fumbles for the exit — escape inches away.10. 
On stage, Victor scans the crowd leisurely… then his gaze 
halts. Sharpens. Locks onto the pillar’s edge like a predator 
sensing prey. 
Victor’s impassive face twists — the corner of his mouth 
curls into a slow, chilling smirk. Dark eyes bore through the 
shadows, pinning Julian in place. 
SHARP, HAWK-LIKE LOCK-ON SOUND. 
Screen FREEZES on Victor’s predatory stare — inescapable, 
hungry.
