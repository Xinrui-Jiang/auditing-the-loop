EPISODE 21: SHATTERED 
INT. JULIAN’S APARTMENT - CONTINUOUS 
Absolute silence fills the empty room. 
Victor walks through tiny living room — steps slow, dread 
building. He scans bare walls, empty closet, stripped bed. 
Eyes lock on small scratched wooden table. 
On table: Titanium Black Card, Milo’s toy car/book, crisp 
white envelope beneath. 
Victor’s hands tremble as he picks up letter. Unfolds it 
carefully. 
Handwriting neat, calm, resolved. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(soft, echoing) 
Don’t look for me, Mr. Grey. 
I am returning you to your perfect 
world. 
Please… wish Milo a healthy and 
happy life for me. 
Goodbye, Victor. 
Victor stares at "Don’t look for me." Breath turns ragged, 
shallow — chest tightens. 
Letter slips from fingers, falls to floor. 
He stumbles back, knees hit worn-out sofa. 
Collapses heavily onto it. Reaches for toy car, clutches it 
tight. 
Leans forward, buries face in hands. 
Low, raw SOB rips out — body shakes. 
VICTOR (V.O.) 
(broken) 
I won the world… and I lost you.32. 
Camera pulls back slowly from Victor alone in empty 
apartment.
