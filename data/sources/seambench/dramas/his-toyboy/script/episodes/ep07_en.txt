EPISODE 7: TRAPPED 
INT. GREY ESTATE - FOYER - NIGHT 
Party winding down. 
Julian stands desperate by massive doors. Milo clings to his 
leg, sobbing hard, shoulders shaking. 
Julian kneels, gently prying Milo’s hands. 
JULIAN 
Milo, baby, don’t cry. 
Mama Ju has surgery tomorrow. 
I really have to go home… 
Milo shakes his head hysterically.12. 
MILO 
No! Mama Ju sleep here! With Dada! 
Victor steps in. Gazes down at Milo, then shifts heavily to 
Julian. 
VICTOR 
My son’s emotional stability is my 
top priority, Doctor. 
Julian looks up, panicked. 
JULIAN 
Mr. Grey, I can’t— 
Victor cuts him off coldly. 
VICTOR 
You’re not leaving tonight, Doc. 
That’s not a request. 
CUT TO: 
INT. GREY ESTATE - MASTER BEDROOM - NIGHT 
Heavy mahogany door CLICKS shut, lock engages. 
Julian is escorted in by silent butler. The suite is vast, 
dimly lit — a gilded cage. 
He stands center, rubbing arms, unsettled. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(breath unsteady) 
Why his room? Not the guest wing. 
He’s doing this on purpose. 
Julian breathes deep, steps into the massive walk-in closet. 
CUT TO: 
INT. GREY ESTATE - MASTER WALK-IN CLOSET - CONTINUOUS 
Rows of Victor’s dark suits. Julian closes eyes, calming his 
heart. 
Low-frequency HUM builds. 
Suddenly — his eyes snap open.13. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
That scent… Cedarwood and bergamot. 
QUICK FLASHBACK 
INT. LUXURY PENTHOUSE - NIGHT 
Victor pinning Julian to bed, face in his neck. 
BACK: 
Julian stumbles, overwhelmed. 
Slow FOOTSTEPS approach from behind. 
Julian freezes. Victor’s tall shadow falls over him — inches 
away, almost touching. 
VICTOR 
(whisper in ear) 
Take a shower. You smell like the 
hospital.
