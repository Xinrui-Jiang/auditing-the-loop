EPISODE 15: THE OUTSIDER 
INT. STREET CORNER CAFE - DAY 
Julian, exhausted in scrubs under cheap coat, sits alone by 
floor-to-ceiling window. Stares blankly at half-empty coffee. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(hollow, exhausted) 
You can’t miss something never 
yours. 
That’s what I tell myself. 
Julian turns to window, gaze freezes on luxury street. Breath 
catches. 
CUT TO: 
EXT. LUXURY BOUTIQUE STREET - CONTINUOUS (JULIAN'S POV 
THROUGH GLASS) 
Victor, handsome in tailored overcoat, exits boutique. 
Beside him: Serena, elegant in designer clothes, holding 
Milo’s hand. 
Serena leans close, laughing. They look like elite family — 
untouchable. 
Music swells, drowning street noise — silent movie torment. 
CUT TO: 
INT. STREET CORNER CAFE - CONTINUOUS 
Julian’s pupils tremble. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(breaking) 
He didn’t tell me he’s back. 
Why would he? 
I’m the dirty secret. She’s the 
future Mrs. Grey. 
Julian looks down. Tear drops on table. Wipes eyes 
aggressively, bitter self-mocking smile.
