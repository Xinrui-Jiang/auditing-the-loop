EPISODE 19: EMPTY APT 
INT. JULIAN’S APARTMENT - DAY 
Rain pours relentlessly against windows, heavy drops pounding 
glass. 
Julian, stripped of his white coat, in simple casual clothes, 
packs last items into suitcase, zips it shut — face 
expressionless. 
He approaches worn wooden table. On it: gleaming titanium 
black card, toy car and book meant for Milo (unsent), and 
unsigned short white farewell letter. 
Julian’s fingers linger on Milo's presents. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(eerily calm, laced with 
ashen despair) 
It’s all over. Back to where I 
belong… 
returning you to your world. 
Julian turns decisively, grabs suitcase, heads to door. No 
backward glance. 
Door CREAKS, then heavy, resolute SLAM. Screen CUTS TO BLACK 
with the sound. 
CUT TO: 
INT. GREY ESTATE - MILO’S BEDROOM - NIGHT 
Soft, warm music box melody plays gently. 
Victor, in dark home shirt, sits bedside. Just finished 
bedtime story, tucks Milo in. 
Milo, clutching plush toy, big eyes full of grievance, voice 
soft but tearful. 
MILO 
Dada… I miss Mama Ju so much.29. 
Victor’s hand pauses. Eyes darken with suppressed pain and 
irritation — believing Julian took the money and left. 
VICTOR 
(restraining emotion, low) 
Shh, he’s just… too busy. No time 
to visit. 
Milo sits up suddenly, tears streaming. 
MILO 
Liar! Why did Aunt Serena make the 
guards throw him out in the rain? 
You all said he’s too busy for me… 
Victor’s pupils contract. Soothing expression freezes, then 
shatters inch by inch. 
VICTOR 
(voice trembling, shocked 
rage building) 
Milo… what did you say? Who threw 
him out in the rain? 
MILO 
(sobbing) 
It was raining so hard that night… 
Mama Ju came to hug me… 
But Aunt Serena said he’s bad, had 
guards drag him out… 
Dada, Mama Ju was crying… does he 
not want me anymore? 
Victor stands sharply. Air around him drops to freezing. Dark 
eyes ignite with bone-chilling, bloodthirsty fury. 
VICTOR 
(gritted teeth) 
Serena…
