EPISODE 1: The Mark 
EXT. MEDICAL UNIVERSITY COMMENCEMENT - STADIUM - DAY 
Bright sunlight. Cheers and camera shutters fill the air. 
Graduates in dark green gowns hurl their caps skyward. 
JULIAN (25), strikingly beautiful yet pale, stands rigid. He 
tugs uncomfortably at the high collar of his gown, frowning. 
EVAN (26) suddenly slings an arm around him and yanks the 
collar open. 
A vivid, dark red bite mark burns on Julian’s neck. 
EVAN 
(grinning wide) 
Whoa! That girl’s a damn tigress! 
Julian quickly yanks the collar back up, forcing an awkward 
smile. 
JULIAN 
(tense) 
Keep it down! Yeah totally...the 
girl yesterday haha... 
SMASH TO FLASHBACK: 
INT. LUXURY PENTHOUSE - NIGHT 
Only cold neon from the city slashes through darkness, 
painting their bodies electric blue and magenta. 
A strong hand — million-dollar watch flashing — RIPS the silk 
tie free. 
VICTOR GREY (32), jacket off, shirt half-unbuttoned, SLAMS 
naked Julian against the floor-to-ceiling glass. 
VICTOR 
(low, dangerous) 
You think you can let someone else 
look at you like that? 
Victor’s teeth sink hard into Julian’s neck — exactly the 
same mark. 
Julian’s head snaps back. A sharp, broken MOAN rips from his 
throat.2. 
Victor yanks him off the glass, spins him, and shoves him 
backward onto silk sheets. He pins Julian down, knees 
slamming on either side of his hips. 
VICTOR (CONT'D) 
(breath hot against his 
ear) 
One more day and you’re free… 
He thrusts in deep — brutal, face-to-face. 
JULIAN 
(gasping) 
Ahh—! 
Victor’s hand locks on Julian’s throat. Hips snap harder. 
JULIAN (CONT'D) 
(moaning shamelessly) 
Fuck… too deep— ahh…! 
…yours… I’m yours—! 
Victor’s dark eyes burn into Julian’s — raw, terrifying 
possession exploding. 
END OF FLASHBACK. 
EXT. MEDICAL UNIVERSITY COMMENCEMENT - STADIUM - DAY 
Deafening applause snaps Julian back. 
The DEAN steps to the microphone, beaming. 
DEAN 
Please welcome our biggest 
benefactor… 
Chairman of Grey Capital, Mr. 
Victor Grey! 
Julian’s head jerks up. Face drains of color, pupils shaking. 
On stage, VICTOR speaks smoothly — but his gaze cuts straight 
through the crowd and locks on Julian. 
His eyes slowly drop to the bite mark. A predatory smirk 
curves his lips. 
Julian clenches his fists until knuckles turn white. His 
expression hardens into cold resolve.3. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
It doesn’t matter. 
After tonight the contract ends. 
I’m taking my freedom back.
