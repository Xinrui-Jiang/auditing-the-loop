EPISODE 11: FROZEN ASSETS 
INT. HOSPITAL CAFETERIA - DAY 
Bustling cafeteria sounds. Sharp BEEP of declined card 
machine. 
Julian, tired in scrubs, at checkout swiping debit card for 
black coffee. 
CASHIER 
(sympathetic) 
Sorry, Dr. Hayes. "Card Frozen. 
Contact Branch." 
Julian frowns, embarrassed. Line behind groans. He checks 
banking app — red exclamation mark. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(sighing) 
Great. Probably a fraud alert. Just 
what I need. 
CUT TO:18. 
INT. GLOBAL BANK - VIP LOUNGE - LATER 
Julian nervous on luxurious leather sofa, out of place amid 
mahogany and art. 
Heavy glass door opens. MANAGER (older, pristine suit) 
enters, sweating, deferential. 
MANAGER 
(bowing slightly, 
trembling awe) 
Dr. Hayes. We apologize for the 
inconvenience. 
Your debit card locked due to… the 
volume of incoming transfers. 
JULIAN 
(confused) 
Transfers? I’m a resident. 
Direct deposit once a month. 
Must be a mistake. 
Manager smiles nervously. Places iPad on table, slides black 
velvet box. It opens — matte-black Titanium Centurion Card. 
MANAGER 
No mistake, sir. Mr. Victor Grey’s 
standing order from four years ago 
was never canceled. 
Julian’s pupils dilate in shock, staring at card like venom. 
MANAGER (O.S.) (CONT'D) 
Monthly allowance continued… Grey 
Capital invested it under your 
name. 
Manager turns iPad. Julian looks — eyes horrified, wide. 
High-pitched RINGING builds, blocking noise. 
INSERT: iPad shows portfolio — over $50,000,000. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(trembling) 
He never let me go. 
I was never free. 
Just a longer leash.
