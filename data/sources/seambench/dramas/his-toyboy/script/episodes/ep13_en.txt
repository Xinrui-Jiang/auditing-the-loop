EPISODE 13: THE INTERLOPER 
INT. LUXURY PENTHOUSE - DAY 
Dead silence broken by sharp phone BUZZ. 
Julian in bathroom, staring at preserved toothbrushes. 
Breathes deep, checks phone. 
INSERT: Secure message from VICTOR GREY: "Emergency crisis in 
London. Grey Capital needs me. Wait for me at the penthouse. 
Don't run this time. - V" 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(shaky breath, relief and 
dread) 
London… I have time to think. 
Time to escape this. 
Soft authoritative elevator DING. 
Sharp stiletto CLICKS on marble. 
Julian hurries to living room, expecting Victor. 
SERENA (28), stunning in blood-red suit, ruthless arrogance. 
Eyes dissect Julian. 
SERENA 
(cold, condescending) 
So. You’re the infamous ghost he’s 
kept the penthouse empty for. 
JULIAN 
(frowning, hostile) 
Who are you? How did you get in?21. 
SERENA 
(stepping forward, owning 
space) 
Serena. Executive VP of Grey 
Capital. 
Victor’s right-hand woman. 
Here for his secure files for the 
London merger. 
Serena walks past to desk, ignoring space. Grabs leather 
folder. Turns, leans, fake venomous smile. 
SERENA (CONT'D) 
(disgust at his clothes) 
A word of advice, Doctor. 
Victor picks up broken things, 
plays savior. 
Julian’s jaw clenches — words strike insecurities. 
Serena steps closer, whisper venomous. 
SERENA (CONT'D) 
Enjoy the golden cage. 
But for Mrs. Grey… he’ll choose an 
equal. 
Someone to rule an empire. Not a 
charity case. 
Serena shoots disdainful look, turns to elevator. 
Julian frozen, fists balled, eyes burning humiliation and 
anger. 
Elevator doors THUD shut.
