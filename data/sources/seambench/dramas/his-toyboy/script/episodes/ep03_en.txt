EPISODE 3: THE FINAL NIGHT5. 
EXT. MEDICAL UNIVERSITY CAMPUS - EVENING 
The campus buzzes with celebrating students and families. 
Laughter and music everywhere. 
In a quiet corner, JULIAN stands alone, graduation gown 
already off. His face is still pale from the shock on stage. 
His phone BUZZES. He checks it — a blank message from the 
usual unknown number. 
Julian takes a deep breath. His eyes harden with cold 
resolve. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
One last time. 
CUT TO: 
INT. LUXURY PENTHOUSE - NIGHT 
Julian pushes the door open. 
But tonight everything is different — warm candlelight glows 
softly. An elegant dining table is set in the center, silver 
dome on a room-service cart beside it. 
VICTOR GREY sits at the table in a perfectly tailored suit — 
He looks up calmly. 
VICTOR 
(gentle, almost soft) 
Sit. You haven’t eaten since the 
ceremony. 
Julian freezes, surprised. He slowly pulls out the chair and 
sits. 
Victor slides a small dark green velvet box across the table. 
It opens with a soft click — revealing a gleaming Rolex 
Daytona. 
VICTOR (CONT'D) 
For your graduation. You earned it. 
For one second, Julian’s eyes soften. He is genuinely 
touched. 
Then his expression turns ice-cold. He pushes the box back.6. 
JULIAN 
(quiet, razor-sharp) 
The contract was for exactly two 
years. 
Today is graduation day. 
I don’t need a severance bonus. 
Victor’s warm facade shatters. His face darkens. 
VICTOR 
(voice turning freezing) 
What the hell are you playing at? 
Name your price. I’ll double it. 
Julian stands without hesitation. He looks down at Victor, 
power completely reversed. 
JULIAN 
(cold and resolute) 
I want my life back. 
We’re even, Mr. Grey. 
Goodbye. 
He turns and walks out. 
Victor rises sharply, eyes wide with shock and brewing storm 
— but he doesn’t move to stop him. 
The door SLAMS shut — heavy, final. 
Victor stands alone. The only sound is the soft ticking of 
the untouched Rolex.
