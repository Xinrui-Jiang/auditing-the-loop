EPISODE 23: LIVE MY LIFE 
EXT. DESERT HIGHWAY - DAY 
Roaring engine of open-top vintage Jeep Wrangler. 
Drone shot: Jeep speeds down endless sun-baked highway, 
towering red rocks, flawless blue sky — vast, open world. 
Harper drives, aviators on, hand steering, other riding wind. 
Julian in passenger seat — hair wind-messed. Looks young, 
vibrant, alive. 
HARPER 
(shouting over wind/music, 
grinning at Julian) 
So, Doc! Two hours driving. 
Still haven’t said… what you 
looking for out here? 
Julian turns to Harper, then endless horizon. 
Pulls old smartphone from backpack — last link to past, 
Victor, Milo. 
Stares at black screen, wind whipping face. 
JULIAN 
(smiling, untethered peace 
in eyes) 
I’m not looking for anything 
anymore, Harper. 
Holds phone out window. Looks one last time.34. 
JULIAN (CONT'D) 
(shouting into wind) 
I’m done living by everyone else’s 
rules!!! 
Done being a secret, or perfect 
little bird! 
SLOW MOTION: Phone slips from fingers, tumbles to asphalt, 
shatters in Jeep’s wake — past left behind. 
Julian faces sun. Laughs loud, unrestrained, joyful — first 
time in series. 
JULIAN (CONT'D) 
(screaming to canyon 
walls, ringing freedom) 
I’m just going to live my own life! 
Camera pushes in on Julian’s face — golden sunlight 
illuminates. No fear, hesitation, sorrow — brightest, 
breathtaking smile of reclaimed soul. 
Frame FREEZES on radiant smile. 
Screen FADES TO WHITE. 
THE END.
