EPISODE 14: BLOCKED 
INT. JULIAN'S APARTMENT - NIGHT 
Rain beats against window. 
Julian sits alone on floor in cramped, dim room — stark 
contrast to Grey Estate. 
He holds small expensive toy car from Milo, thumb rubbing it 
gently. Eyes red-rimmed, exhausted.22. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(breaking, tearful) 
Three weeks since Victor left for 
London. 
Three weeks since I held him. 
Why does it hurt? He’s not even 
mine… 
Julian grabs phone. Scrolls to Victor’s name — finger hovers, 
trembling. Doesn’t call. 
Taps "Emma (Nanny)" instead. 
Phone rings twice. CLICK. 
JULIAN 
(desperate, hopeful) 
Emma? It’s Julian. 
How’s Milo? Is he asleep? 
Chilling scoff over phone. 
SERENA (V.O.) 
(icy condescension) 
Emma is indisposed, Doctor. This is 
Serena. 
Julian’s face pales. Grip tightens on phone. 
JULIAN 
(hardening, powerless) 
Let me speak to Milo. Just a 
minute. 
SERENA (V.O.) 
(dramatic sigh) 
Milo is a Grey. Heir training — 
etiquette, languages, riding. 
No time for distractions… or 
playdates with hired help. 
Julian’s breath hitches — words stab deep. 
SERENA (V.O.) (CONT'D) 
(venomous whisper) 
Stop calling. Know your place. 
Sharp BEEP — hang up. 
Julian lowers dead phone. Pulls knees to chest, curls into 
ball. Presses toy to forehead — single tear slips down cheek.23.
