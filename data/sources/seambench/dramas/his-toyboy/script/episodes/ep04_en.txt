EPISODE 4: DADA'S WIFE 
TITLE CARD: BOLD WHITE TEXT ON BLACK 
"4 YEARS LATER." 
CUT TO: 
INT. HOSPITAL CORRIDOR - DAY 
Bright, sterile light. 
JULIAN (29), crisp white doctor’s coat, “Surgical Resident” 
badge, walks briskly, reading a chart. 
Tiny footsteps patter.7. 
MILO (3) — miniature expensive suit, eyes exactly like 
Victor’s — runs out and throws himself around Julian’s leg. 
MILO 
(bright, loud) 
Mama! 
Julian freezes. His hand hovers mid-air. 
JULIAN 
(stammering, mortified) 
Hey there. I… I’m a man, little 
one. I’m Doctor Ju. 
Milo clings tighter, burying his face in the scrubs. 
Footsteps click sharply. ELEANOR GREY (60, elegant, 
intimidating matriarch) hurries in with a flustered nanny, 
EMMA (24). 
EMMA 
Milo! Doctor, I’m so sorry, he just 
— 
Milo whips around, puffs out his tiny chest, and shouts to 
the entire corridor: 
MILO 
(innocent, deafening) 
Milo has no mommy! Dada’s wife is 
Mama Ju! 
Dada love Mama Ju — make baby 
together! 
Dead silence. Nurses freeze. Jaws drop. Eleanor is 
speechless. 
Eleanor’s face flashes shock, then she forces a tight, 
graceful smile. 
ELEANOR 
(flustered but perfectly 
composed) ) 
Oh dear… children and their wild 
imaginations! 
Come now, Milo, we’re terribly 
late. 
She swiftly scoops Milo up (he whines in protest) and strides 
away down the corridor, nanny trailing behind.8. 
Julian’s face burns deep red. He wants to disappear into the 
floor. 
SMASH CUT TO: 
INT. GREY ESTATE STUDY - DAY 
Luxurious dark wood. 
Milo pouts on a huge leather sofa. 
VICTOR GREY (36), even more commanding and dangerously 
handsome, kneels before his son, wiping a tear from Milo’s 
cheek. His eyes hold hidden, obsessive fire. 
VICTOR 
(low, tender, but steel 
underneath) 
Okay, buddy. Dada promises… 
I will bring Mama Ju home to marry 
him. 
QUICK CUT TO: 
INT. HOSPITAL LOCKER ROOM - DAY 
Exhausted Julian opens a sleek black velvet envelope left on 
his bench. He pulls out a gold-foiled card. 
INVITATION (ON CARD) 
You are cordially invited to 
Milo Grey’s 3rd Birthday Party. 
Julian stares at the name “Grey.” His breath catches. Pure 
terror flashes across his face. 
PAYWALL
