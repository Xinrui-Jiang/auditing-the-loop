EPISODE 17: POISON 
INT. HOSPITAL - JULIAN’S OFFICE - DAY 
Sterile hospital hum. 
Julian at small desk, drained, rubbing temples. 
On desk: beautifully illustrated children’s book. He traces 
cover gently. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(exhausted) 
I promised I’d come back. 
Have to get this to him… 
Sharp arrogant CLICK — door locks from inside. 
Julian looks up, startled. Serena, in pristine white suit, 
leans against door, clinical coldness. 
Julian stands abruptly, defensive. 
JULIAN 
Get out. You have no right here.26. 
Serena walks slowly to desk, chillingly calm. 
SERENA 
Not here to fight, Doctor. 
Here for reality. You’re medical— 
let’s talk psychology. 
Eyes flick to book, mocking scoff. 
SERENA (CONT'D) 
(leaning in, razor sharp) 
Milo’s Grey heir. Needs traditional 
family: strong father, respectable 
mother. 
Julian’s face tightens, breathing shallow. 
SERENA (CONT'D) 
(venomous whisper) 
You’re a man, Julian. Playing 
‘Mommy’ causes identity issues, 
makes him high society 
laughingstock. You’re destroying 
his future. 
Julian’s color drains — clinical manipulation paralyzes. 
SERENA (CONT'D) 
(straightening, fatal) 
If you love him… get out of his 
life. Forever. 
Serena turns, walks out, door open. 
Julian alone in silence. Trembling hand touches book. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(broken agony) 
She’s right. I’m not saving him… 
I’m ruining him. 
Julian’s legs give out. Sinks into chair, clutches book to 
chest. Curls forward, face buried, shoulders shake with gut
wrenching sobs — defense shattered.
