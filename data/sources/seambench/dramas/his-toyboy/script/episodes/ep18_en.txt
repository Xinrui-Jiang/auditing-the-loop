EPISODE 18: THE DECISION27. 
EXT. GREY ESTATE - GATES - LATE AFTERNOON 
Massive wrought-iron gates loom against the golden hues of 
the setting sun, casting long shadows that stretch like 
barriers across the driveway. 
Julian, hidden in deep shadows of stone pillar, in simple 
trench coat, collar up. 
His face is a mask of exhaustion etched with quiet 
determination, eyes hollow from sleepless nights but 
strangely serene in resignation. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(soft resignation) 
She was right. The only way to 
protect you… 
is to disappear. 
A gentle wind rustles the leaves nearby. 
CUT TO: 
EXT. GREY ESTATE - GARDENS - CONTINUOUS (JULIAN'S POV THROUGH 
BARS) 
In distance, sunlit lawn: Milo plays with toy. Victor stands 
nearby, hands in pockets, watching calmly. 
Julian rests forehead on cold bars. Tear slides down cheek. 
Despite tear, lips curve into trembling, loving, heartbroken 
smile — memorizing Milo’s face. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(breaking sob) 
I can’t give you the world, Milo. 
Not the empires, not the legacy. 
But I can give you a perfect future 
— clean, untainted. 
You deserve that. You deserve 
everything I could never be. 
Inside garden, Milo stops playing. Looks curiously and 
hopefully toward gates. 
Julian’s breath catches. Steps back, hides in shadows. 
JULIAN (V.O.) (CONT'D) 
(whispering farewell) 
Goodbye, my sweet baby.28. 
Julian turns back to gates. Expression shifts: sorrow to 
dead-eyed resolution. Pulls coat tighter, walks down empty 
road. 
He doesn't look back. Not even once.
