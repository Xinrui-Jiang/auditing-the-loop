EPISODE 12: THE SHRINE19. 
INT. LUXURY PENTHOUSE HALLWAY - DAY 
Soft elevator DING. Heavy, hesitant footsteps. 
Julian stands before imposing double doors, breathing hard — 
anger, confusion, fear mixing. 
He opens black velvet box from bank. 
Under Titanium Black Card: sleek matte-black keycard. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
He left the keycard. He knew I’d 
come. Knew I couldn’t walk away 
from this… insanity. 
Julian presses card to lock. Sharp BEEP, heavy CLICK. 
He pushes door open, steps into darkness. 
CUT TO: 
INT. LUXURY PENTHOUSE - CONTINUOUS 
Cold filtered sunlight bathes the space. 
Julian enters, eyes widening in shock. 
He walks through living room — everything unchanged from four 
years ago. A frozen museum, time capsule, shrine. 
JULIAN 
(whispering) 
What… what is this? 
Into en-suite bathroom. Approaches marble sink. 
On counter, spotless beside Victor’s toothbrush: Julian’s 
cheap, faded plastic one — preserved like a relic. 
Julian stumbles back, hand over mouth. 
CUT TO: 
INT. GREY CAPITAL - BOARDROOM - DAY 
Victor at head of glass table. Terrified executives present, 
sweating under his cold aura. 
Private phone VIBRATION buzzes.20. 
Screen lights: ALERT: PENTHOUSE SUITE 01 - UNLOCKED (GUEST: 
JULIAN HAYES) 
Executives freeze. Victor stares at phone. 
His emotionless eyes ignite with dangerous fire. Dark, 
victorious smirk curls. 
VICTOR 
(low, to himself) 
Welcome home, little bird.
