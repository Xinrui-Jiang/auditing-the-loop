EPISODE 8: THROUGH THE GLASS 
INT. GREY ESTATE - MASTER BEDROOM / BATHROOM - NIGHT 
Steam curls thick and hot from the slightly ajar bathroom 
door. Running water hums seductively, filling the air with 
humid warmth. 
Julian stands rigid in the bedroom, arms hugged tight to his 
chest. 
VICTOR (O.S.) 
(deep, commanding through 
the water) 
Julian. Bring me a towel. 
Julian’s breath hitches. Eyes wide, he spots plush white 
towels on the chaise. Swallows hard, grabs one, and edges 
toward the bathroom — pulse racing. 
CUT TO: 
INT. GREY ESTATE - MASTER BATHROOM - CONTINUOUS 
Suffocating steam envelops the space. Frosted glass encloses 
the massive shower, fogged and glowing.14. 
Through it, Victor’s powerful silhouette looms — broad 
shoulders, sculpted back, water tracing every hard line of 
his body. 
Julian approaches trembling, face flushed from the heat. Eyes 
averted, he extends the towel around the glass edge. 
JULIAN 
(voice shaky) 
Here… take it. 
Water shuts off abruptly. BASS BOOM. 
A dripping hand shoots out — grabs Julian’s wrist in a vice, 
wet and scorching. 
Julian gasps. 
Victor steps half-out, water sluicing down his chiseled 
chest, abs glistening, towel forgotten. With power, he yanks 
Julian forward and SLAMS him against the cold tiled wall. 
Julian’s back arches against the tiles, trapped by Victor’s 
wet, half-naked frame — steam wrapping them. 
VICTOR 
(leaning in, smirk dark) 
Why so shy, Julian? 
Victor’s eyes burn. Thumb traces Julian’s throbbing pulse, 
slow and teasing. 
VICTOR (CONT'D) 
It’s not like you haven’t tasted 
every inch of me. 
Julian’s face flames crimson. 
He wrenches free violently, towel dropping wet to the floor. 
Bolts from the bathroom like prey fleeing. 
Fast RETREATING FOOTSTEPS. 
Victor leans against the frosted glass, picking up the towel. 
Watches the doorway with a satisfied smile.
