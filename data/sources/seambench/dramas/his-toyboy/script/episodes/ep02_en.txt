EPISODE 2: THE PRICE 
EXT. MEDICAL UNIVERSITY COMMENCEMENT - STADIUM - DAY 
Roaring crowd explodes in applause. Balloons and caps fly. 
JULIAN stands motionless in cap and gown, face blank. Cheers 
distort into high-pitched RINGING. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
They see a billionaire’s generous 
donation. 
I see the man who bought my soul… 
because I had no other choice. 
SMASH CUT TO: 
INT. LUXURY PENTHOUSE - NIGHT 
Cold neon slices through darkness, painting bodies in 
electric blue and violet. 
Rain hammers windows like a frantic heartbeat. 
VICTOR GREY, jacket gone, shirt half-unbuttoned, SLAMS naked 
Julian against silk sheets. Pins wrists above his head, body 
covering him. 
Julian’s back arches. Knuckles bone-white clawing sheets. 
JULIAN 
(eyes shut, broken moan) 
Ahh— too much… 
Victor growls low. Hips snap forward — deep, claiming thrust. 
JULIAN (CONT'D) 
(voice cracking) 
Oh god— yes… right there…! 
Wet skin slaps. Heavy breathing tangles with the storm. 
Julian’s body jerks with every stroke. Desperate moan rips 
out.4. 
Victor grips Julian’s chin, forcing eyes to lock. 
VICTOR 
(dangerous whisper) 
Look at me. 
Julian stares up. Flicker of struggle… then surrender. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
My parents died in a car crash. 
Bank came for the house before we'd 
even buried them... 
A tear slips from Julian's lashes. 
JULIAN (V.O.) (CONT'D) 
I worked shifts after shifts at a 
club until he offered to make it 
all disappear… for a price. 
Julian closes his eyes. The tear slips down, soaking into the 
pillow. 
CUT TO: 
The cold sound of a running shower echoes from the bathroom. 
Julian lies wrecked on bed, half-covered, eyes empty. 
Victor emerges in towel. Indifferently dresses, fastens 
million-dollar watch with sharp CLICK. No glance back. 
Door SLAMS — heavy, heartless. 
Julian opens eyes. Softness gone. Ice-cold clarity. 
JULIAN 
(to himself, calm) 
Just one more month… until 
graduation. 
Ticking clock grows louder. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
And I’ll finally be free.
