EPISODE 16: STOLEN PROMISE 
INT. GREY ESTATE - BACK CORRIDOR - NIGHT 
Julian, soaking wet, out of breath, sneaks through dim 
servant’s corridor — terrified but resolute. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(frantic panic) 
Emma called. Milo hasn’t eaten in 
two days. 
Fever spiking. I don’t care about 
rules. I have to see him. 
CUT TO: 
INT. GREY ESTATE - GRAND STAIRCASE - CONTINUOUS 
Julian peeks into opulent foyer. Tiny figure on bottom marble 
step: Milo , flushed with fever, eyes swollen, clutching toy 
car. 
JULIAN 
(gasping, heart 
shattering) 
Milo… 
Milo looks up, lip quivering. 
MILO 
(weak, raspy) 
Mama Ju…? You came back. 
Julian kneels, pulls feverish Milo into desperate embrace. 
Both sob. Milo clings, face in Julian’s neck. 
JULIAN 
(sobbing, kissing Milo’s 
head) 
I’m here, baby. I’m sorry. 
I promise… I’ll always come back 
for you. I promise. 
MILO 
Milo misses Mama Ju...Dada.. 
Before Milo finishes, sharp designer heels CLICK, CLACK 
approach.25. 
SERENA (O.S.) 
(disgust, icy authority) 
How pathetic. Stray dog through the 
back door? 
Julian freezes, shielding Milo. 
Serena at top of stairs, looking down like garbage. Two 
massive guards flank her. 
SERENA (CONT'D) 
(dead, ruthless to guards) 
Get this trespasser out. Sanitize 
what he touched. 
Guards grab Julian’s arms, tear him from screaming Milo. 
MILO 
(hysterical, reaching) 
NO! Don’t take him! Mama Ju! 
SLOW MOTION: Julian dragged backward to oak doors. Face pale, 
rain-teared, eyes locked on sobbing Milo — agony of 
powerlessness devastating.
