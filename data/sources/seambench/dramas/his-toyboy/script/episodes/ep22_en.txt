EPISODE 22: THE JOURNEY 
Victor’s SOB lingers. 
Sudden WHOOSH of wind — upbeat warm acoustic indie-folk music 
kicks in. 
SMASH CUT TO: 
EXT. WESTERN NATIONAL PARK (E.G., ZION OR GRAND CANYON) - DAY 
Breathtaking drone shot: sun-drenched canyons explode in 
golden, vibrant earthy tones. 
Julian, at scenic overlook edge — simple white t-shirt, open 
flannel, hiking backpack. Wind ruffles hair. 
JULIAN (V.O.) 
(clear, faint sorrow) 
Three thousand miles away. 
Different time zone. 
Different world. 
Julian closes eyes, breathes deep crisp pine air. Chest rises 
— weight lifts from shoulders. 
Crunch of hiking boots on gravel. 
HARPER (O.S.) 
(confident, breezy) 
First time seeing the canyon? 
HARPER (27), sun-kissed in hiking gear, bandana, aviators in 
hair — cool, independent energy — walks up. 
JULIAN 
(small polite smile) 
Obvious? 
HARPER 
(chuckling, leans on 
railing) 
I’m Harper. 
Your guide for the week. 
Yeah, obvious. ‘City boy running 
from broken heart’ vibe. 
Julian’s smile falters briefly. Pain flashes — ghosts of 
Victor and Milo.33. 
Harper notices silence. Playfully bumps his shoulder. 
HARPER (CONT'D) 
Hey. Whatever you left in concrete 
jungle… 
canyon’s big enough to swallow it. 
Welcome to the wild, Julian. 
Julian looks at Harper. 
JULIAN 
(steady, fragile genuine 
smile) 
Thank you. I think… I’m going to 
like it here.
