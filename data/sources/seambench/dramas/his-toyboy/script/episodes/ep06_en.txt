EPISODE 6: MINE 
INT. GREY ESTATE - BALLROOM - CONTINUOUS 
Julian, pale and desperate, whirls toward a side door — 
shadows swallowing his retreat. 
PAIGE (27), glamorous in red, blocks him abruptly. Finger 
presses his chest, flirtatious grin. 
PAIGE 
Leaving already, handsome? 
Let’s slip out together. 
Julian recoils, body rigid. 
JULIAN 
No—excuse me. 
A piercing CHILD’S CRY slices the air. 
MILO (O.S.) 
NOOOOOOOOO! 
Milo (3) barrels in, tears streaming. Shoves Paige’s leg, 
clings to Julian. 
MILO (CONT'D) 
Don’t touch! He’s Dada’s! Bad 
woman! 
Paige falters, forced smile cracking. 
PAIGE 
Milo, sweetie, I’m just— 
Oppressive FOOTSTEPS — STEP… STEP… Crowd freezes.11. 
VICTOR (O.S.) 
(deep, arctic cold) 
) 
Is there a problem here, Paige? 
Victor parts the guests like waves. Scoops Milo up. Milo sobs 
into his neck. 
MILO 
Dada… she touched Mama Ju… 
Victor pats Milo’s back gently and glares at Paige — she 
blanches. 
Then his eyes lock on Julian, predatory. 
Then Victor turns slowly — dark eyes locking onto Julian like 
a predator’s snare. 
VICTOR 
(soft, iron-clad) 
My son is very protective 
of what belongs to this family. 
Steps forward, cornering Julian. 
VICTOR (CONT'D) 
(double-edged command) 
And he is absolutely right. 
Don’t. Touch. Him. 
BASS BOOM. FREEZE on Victor’s gaze and Julian’s trapped face.
